# Robin's Wheel and Tweaks v2.0.0

## Changes

- Successful Ocarina songs now play the bow-and-arrow combo sound. Ocarina mode exits after the one-second cue delay, when Song of Healing takes effect.
- Hawk Grass now verifies and reloads its model resource when needed and avoids starting the whistle from an invalid or stale player state.
- Added original Hawk Grass, Shield Surf, and Goron Tunic icons.

## Known limitations

- Twilit Essentials Items v1.1 has no hover/selection callback or non-assignable-item option. Ice Arrow remains assignable in the Modded page; its bow-and-arrow combo prompt is supported only in the legacy wheel.
- Custom item effects are not replicated to players who do not run this mod.
