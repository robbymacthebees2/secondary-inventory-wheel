# Secondary Inventory Wheel

Made by RobinTheSoundsmith. This Dusklight code mod adds a second item wheel with 24 mod-owned item slots, Ice Leaf, Shield Surf, a Sit action, and a separate Goron Tunic slot. The combined build also includes the Crests of Courage co-op runtime for player, pose, and actor synchronization. It reuses the vanilla item-wheel presentation while handling slot identity, selection, movement, and rendering independently of vanilla inventory indexes. Hawk Grass is provided in the first secondary slot and Hover Boots in the second; both can be assigned to X or Y, or Z when the optional Z item slot is enabled.

## Repository contents

- `src/mod.cpp` owns initialization, hook registration, and shared private state. Feature implementations are organized under `src/features/`, including the authorized Fairy Companion port in `fairy_companion.cpp`.
- `mod.json` contains the Dusklight mod manifest.
- `CMakeLists.txt` defines the mod target for Dusklight's CMake build.
- `src/coop/` contains the integrated Co-op runtime; its lifecycle is called by the single Wheel mod entry point.
- `res/models/` contains shipped player-model resources, including two Zora Link variants. The game's models are unpacked into this mod's data directory for the existing skin loader.
- Co-op voice chat uses Opus and the platform audio APIs; Windows and Linux builds use Dusklight's SDL3 audio runtime for microphone capture, while Android and Apple builds are listen-only. The Hero of Time voice pack is included with its attribution in the in-game Credits tab.
- `third_party/crests-of-courage/` preserves the Co-op source license and full source credits. Third-party models are not covered by that MIT license; in-game credits identify their authors.
- `res/` is the mod's resource directory. The wheel loads its presentation from Dusklight at runtime and includes custom wheel icons and shipped player models. The Deku Nut itself renders the native slingshot nut model from the game's `Alink` archive.
- The Fairy Companion integration keeps the flying companion, color and appearance selection, optional light, and stationary A-button Call/Put Away toggle; its time-slow, freeze, explosion, and ability HUD features are intentionally excluded.

## Dawnlight integration

The Dark Link item keeps its secondary-wheel slot and X/Y assignment, while its toggle directly enables Dawnlight's dark outfit lighting, self-lit red eyes, and flowing body-aura visual without the Wheel's former wipe transition, warp effect, sound, or combat multipliers. It uses the current outfit and textures; the Dark Link visual, aura mesh, and shaders are based on Dawnlight by BeZide93 under CC0, with credit and license in the package. The optional Z item slot supports native inventory items and assignable secondary-wheel items, with assignments saved per game file, and displays the selected Bow's arrows, Slingshot seeds, bomb inventory, or Lantern oil meter. While enabled, Midna moves to D-pad Left, the secondary wheel automatically opens with Down, and the map remains on Right; the configured wheel direction resumes when Z is disabled. Disable Dawnlight's own Z Item Slot to avoid competing controls.

Bow aiming adds Vanilla, 3rd Person, and Cinema camera modes, optional aim movement, a configurable Cinema zoom and 3rd Person reticle height, and arrow direction correction toward the reticle. Vanilla aim hides Link's body model while aiming; other camera modes are unchanged. The 3rd Person camera follows the game's native Bow aim yaw and pitch, including during midair aim; the held Bow pose is corrected during model calculation without changing Link's gameplay body angles. Held arrows update every frame so their pose follows Link's aim during Bullet Time; fired arrows remain slowed. Ground and airborne Bow aiming use Dawnlight's Bow Subject aiming flow, including its stick-driven movement blend, native camera/body pitch setup, camera-centered sight, and midair Bullet Time aim update. Airborne manual-jump aim is prepared only after a Bow button is activated during the jump; ordinary jumps with the Bow equipped do not force aim. Activating an assigned Bow in midair skips the equip animation and enters aim immediately; doing so while gliding releases the glide first. Bullet Time can be Off, Always, or BOTW (requires twice the native standing-jump height in ground clearance). During a manual jump, requesting a selected Bow auto-equips and aims it; Link movement and non-Link actors run at 10% speed and fired arrows at 20% for up to five seconds. Assigned Bombs drop from the manual glide. The Z slot supports native Bow combinations with Bombs and Hawk Eye; while viewing a compatible item on the vanilla wheel, press R to combine or uncombine it with the Z Bow. Bomb Arrow and Hawk Arrow combo counters use the compact Z-slot layout. Dawnlight's native Gale Boomerang model and tornado sound provide the Revali's Gale charge-ready and launch gust effects. Dusklight records and interpolates rendered model/joint matrices normally during Bullet Time, but the Wheel throttles actor simulation to 10% (20% for fired arrows); skipped simulation updates are not synthesized into extra animation poses, and the interpolation service has no API to force intermediate AI, collision, or animation states. The Wheel does not add Dawnlight's audio time-stretch.

The Dawnlight Sprint port supports human and Wolf Link, with separate enable and speed settings, and carries sprint momentum into manual jumps. For human Sprint, releasing the Roll button within a short input window starts a roll; keeping it held past the window starts Sprint instead. Optional Wheel stamina drains while sprinting and recovers afterward; when Twilit Essentials stamina is enabled, the Wheel uses that system and hides its own meter. Dawnlight's Disable Auto Jump option is also used: it suppresses automatic ledge jumps without disabling normal falls or ledge grabs.

Optional Flurry Rush detects a close enemy attack during a locked-on dodge, briefly slows Link and nearby enemies, prevents the triggering hit from damaging Link, and moves Link into sword range when the counterattack starts. The Enemy Spawner offers 43 Dawnlight enemy profiles for solo testing; it is unavailable during co-op, pause, events, or scene transitions. Arrow Damage Multiplier adjusts arrow power while Bullet Time is active (100% by default, configurable from 0% to 1000%). OOT Style Hearts optionally replaces the heart-meter textures and is off by default. Flurry Rush and OOT Style Hearts are also off by default.

The Fairy Companion settings let players select Navi, Tatl, Tael, or the extra green color and toggle its colored night/indoor light. A separate appearance setting offers **Fairy**, **Midna (Shadow)**, and **Midna (Regular)**. Both Midna appearances use her human-form follow idle animation (`ANM_LEADWAIT`, resource `0x01CB`) and the native Hair1-Hair5 joint pose; the separate hair model is anchored at Hair5 with the game's 6.5-unit offset. Regular uses the game's full-color Midna models and textures shown while riding on Wolf Link, while Shadow uses the shadow appearance. Midna follows at a lower height with a 135-unit cap relative to Link, while retaining the companion's follow and toggle behavior. The companion does not spawn or control the story Midna actor. Co-op sends the companion's appearance, position, and visibility so other players can see it. The companion follows Link and flies around a locked-on target. When Link is standing still on the ground with no weapon or item drawn, A shows **Call** or **Put Away** to toggle the companion; the game-native fairy sound plays on either transition. The Fairy appearance reuses the game's model, animation, and particles. Fairy abilities are not included. The glider uses the texture from Dawnlight Custom Glider by BeZide; its model geometry is unchanged.

Wheel feature files are included by `src/mod.cpp` and built as one translation unit to preserve private state and hook wiring. Co-op runtime files are separate translation units in `src/coop/` and are added to the same native plugin target; the combined package has one Wheel manifest and one mod entry point.

## Building

This mod is built from a Dusklight source checkout, which provides the SDK, headers, libraries, and `add_mod` CMake function. Place this directory at `mods/secondary_inventory_wheel` and ensure the checkout's top-level `CMakeLists.txt` includes:

```cmake
add_subdirectory(mods/secondary_inventory_wheel)
```

Configure Dusklight as usual, then build the `secondary_inventory_wheel_package` target. The build produces `secondary_inventory_wheel.dusk`.

## Slot model

Poke Bombs dropped during manual gliding fall to the ground before resuming their native Bombling behavior.

The Deku Nut follows the Armos Hammer in the secondary wheel. It uses the game's native bomb actor for carrying and throwing, but renders at double size with a small upward offset using the native slingshot nut model from Link's already-loaded `Alink` archive. Each tracked nut has its own model instance so rapid throws cannot overwrite another nut's pending draw transform. Press its assigned X/Y button again to throw it; while manual Cucco gliding, pressing the assigned button drops a Deku Nut. The custom BMD model is not loaded at runtime because it caused crashes in the game's BMD loader. Its fuse is disabled so it only pops on enemy contact or a surface impact. On impact, it uses the standard sword-hit particle effect and freezes living enemies and bosses within 350 game units for five seconds without applying bomb damage. A custom Deku Nut icon is used on the wheel and X/Y HUD.

Secondary X/Y assignments are saved per game file and only persisted when that file is saved. Assignments from earlier releases are not migrated; reassign items and save each file to record them.

The Dark Link item applies Dawnlight's near-black outfit lighting and highlights while preserving loaded outfit textures and material detail, plus self-lit red eyes and flowing aura ribbons. Toggle it from its assigned X/Y button; it changes the appearance directly and does not replace the outfit, alter Link's movement, or change damage. Its body and eye appearance is synchronized to remote players in Co-op and Hyrule Online. The local aura follows Link's current pose, detaches and drifts upward, respects scene depth, and also appears in the Collection preview.

The secondary wheel has 24 mod-owned item slots (`0x80` through `0x97`), followed by dedicated Ice Leaf (`0xFB`), **Shield Surf** (`0xFC`), **Sit** (`0xFD`), and Goron Tunic (`0xFE`) slots. Its opening and closing pose and fade use Dusklight's frame interpolation, and stick navigation advances one slot per the vanilla cursor-change wait. Custom item IDs and the dedicated slot IDs are private to the mod and are never written into the game's vanilla X/Y item assignments. Secondary X/Y assignments are stored with each game save: changes take effect immediately but are recorded only when that save is saved, and loading a save restores the assignments from that save. The optional Z item slot adds a third per-save assignment shared by the vanilla and secondary wheels; enabling it moves Midna's trigger from Z to D-pad Left. Assigning an item to Z moves that item from any existing X/Y assignment, matching the game's one-button-per-item behavior. Assigning a secondary item to X, Y, or Z leaves that button's vanilla assignment intact; while a secondary item is assigned, it takes precedence for that button's HUD icon and activation. Assigning a vanilla item to that same button from the vanilla wheel clears its private secondary assignment so the vanilla item immediately takes over. Secondary items do not replace Wolf Link's X/Y abilities, inputs, or HUD prompts while transformed. When a vanilla Lantern, Bow, Slingshot, or bomb type is assigned to Z, its HUD shows the current oil, arrow, seed, or bomb count. Shield Surf uses Link's currently equipped shield as the board and reuses the native Ice Leaf board movement on walkable ground or the stable Hover Boots plane; it can transition from ordinary ground onto the hover plane without dismounting, and Link stays at the plane using the board-riding pose instead of starting the Hover Boots landing sequence. The pointed top of the shield faces forward. Assign it to X or Y, press the button to start, and press it again to dismount. Its temporary board actor is kept in the native ride-only mode so it cannot order the Ice Leaf pickup event, including on collisions, and is deleted as ride state is cleared. Starting a get-item event or cutscene ends Shield Surf and clears its ride state so Link can move normally afterward. It does not replace the vanilla Ice Leaf item or alter normal Ice Leaf behavior. Hawk Grass uses its private item ID in the first slot and displays a custom Hawk Grass icon. Its assigned X/Y/Z action enters the game's grass-whistle flow, retaining the native reed melody and hawk sound effects. After its attack and return flight finish, the Hawk called by modded Hawk Grass is removed after five seconds in idle with Link outside the Hawk interaction. Hover Boots use their private item ID in the second slot and a custom boots-with-wings icon in the wheel, X/Y preview, and HUD. The X/Y HUD icon is rendered directly from the mod resource, without using a vanilla item ID to load or scale it. Assign them to X, Y, or Z and press that button to toggle airwalking immediately; the Iron Boots equip animation plays on ground and in the air but does not gate the Hover Boots state change or modify vanilla Iron Boots. Hover Boots are automatically unequipped on successful area transitions. While active, the magnetic-boots particles follow Link’s foot joints immediately, including while he is still standing on solid ground. When Link walks or jumps off solid ground, Hover Boots keep him at the height where he left it and show a ripple beneath him. While airborne, the mod prevents vanilla auto-fall/ledge actions from replacing the current player action, including idle movement states, while allowing other mods' jump actions to run. At the hover plane it uses a one-shot landing animation and returns Link to movement without requiring a background polygon. The Sit slot displays a text-based **SIT** icon and uses Link's gameplay `WAIT_SIT` animation. While seated, it keeps Link at the current floor height, tilts his body to walkable floor slopes, lowers the model 28 units toward the surface, and offsets the rendered model 65 units forward in Link's facing direction without moving ground collision. The leg pose itself remains the one authored in `WAIT_SIT`; straightening it would require a custom animation. Press the assigned X/Y button again to restore the normal standing idle. Movement input is suppressed while seated. Manual jump uses the game's native auto-jump action from grounded walking or idle and can also jump from the Hover Boots' stable hover plane; its default A binding is configurable and replaces that button's normal action only while jump conditions are met. The normal A-button put-away behavior for vanilla held items is unchanged outside those conditions. The Dawnlight Dark Link visual is provided in the third secondary slot with its own shadow-warrior icon in the wheel, X/Y preview, and HUD. Press its assigned button to toggle the appearance; assigning it does not activate it. Wolf Link is unchanged. Ice Arrow appears directly after Dark Link in the wheel with its custom blue arrowhead icon; it is an informational bow feature, not an item to assign to X or Y. With a bow equipped, hovering over Ice Arrow shows the game's native R-button combo prompt and flashing combo indicator. Press R to toggle Ice Arrow mode on or off; the selected arrow is spawned with Ice Arrow's type while the mode is on. After updating from older versions, assign secondary items again in the secondary wheel; the old implementation mirrored those assignments into vanilla item state.

The mod settings remain available in the Mods panel and can also be opened directly from the menu bar's **Robin's W&T** entry. Players can choose which Left/Down D-pad direction opens the secondary wheel and disable that binding independently, and optionally enable the Z item slot (off by default; moves Midna to D-pad Left). Manual Jump has an enable toggle, a **Jump Button** setting (R, L (LB), R3, or L3; R by default), and a **Jump Height** setting from 100% to 500%. **Disable Auto Jump** independently prevents the game's automatic ledge jump while preserving ordinary falls and ledge grabs. **Glide** can be enabled separately and uses the selected **Glide Item** (Cucco or Dawnlight's cloth Glider); press the jump button again while airborne to deploy it, and it is put away when Link lands. A separate L1/L2/R1/R2/L3/R3 bind (R3 by default) lowers the Hover Boots plane by one vanilla standing-jump height.

The Hyrule Online options include a **Players Online** group entry that opens a live list of player names; press B to return to the options panel. The menu also lets players walk on lava indefinitely with the Goron Tunic (without sinking or triggering the five-second lava return), toggle Twilight Ward, enable one-hit damage for attacks from the Hawk called by the secondary Hawk Grass, toggle Hover Boots water walking, enable quarter-heart-per-second healing while Link sits (off by default; begins after four seconds and uses the spring-healing sound), and select music to play while sitting. **File Select** is currently the only Sit music option; it uses the game's native File Select BGM sequence. When Link sits, the current area soundtrack fades out before File Select starts through the main BGM route, replacing the area sequence. The current scene's wave archives are preserved, and the original archive state and main sequence are restored when Link stands or Sit ends. The selected sequence bypasses the fanfare queue, and battle-managed BGM is held off for the duration so it cannot interrupt the track.

While Goron Tunic is active, Link must wear the Hero's Tunic and its cap, body, and skirt are temporarily colored `#FF0000` through the co-op color system; activating it while wearing Zora Armor, Magic Armor, or Ordon Clothes is blocked. Infinite lava walking overrides the heavy-armor sinking surface state while Link is on lava. Vanilla recalculates that state during room setup, so the mod corrects it after that calculation; when lava walking is disabled, lava under heavy armor is also kept out of the quicksand-return path that expects a sand-only transform buffer, allowing the game's magma-return logic to handle it. Twilight Ward is off by default and, while Link wears Magic Armor, normal and heavy hits still deal their usual damage but do not interrupt Link, stun him, or knock him back. Water walking is off by default and only applies while the secondary-wheel Hover Boots are equipped; it keeps movement on a flat water-level plane instead of using submerged floor slopes, drops, or ice-slip movement. It also suppresses submerged slide-polygon and slope checks and clears the vanilla ground-step offsets while the water plane is active. Enabling water walking replaces airwalking, so players can choose which Hover Boots behavior they prefer. With the one-hit option enabled, player targets take 20 hearts of damage. The modded Hawk's attack is classified as a thrown object, normal sword hit, and bomb for compatibility with enemy-specific damage handlers, including mounted Bulblins.

The menu also provides Rupee Magnet as an optional feature rather than a wheel item. Its radius defaults to 100% (300 game units, matching the game's existing rupee-return distance) and can be set from 50% (150 units) to 300% (900 units) in 25% steps. A separate off-by-default toggle lets Rupee Magnet collect the STAR/clawshot minigame orbs within that radius, and only while a minigame is active. Lava walking, Twilight Ward, Hawk one-hit damage, Hover Boots water walking, and Rupee Magnet are off by default. Twilight Ward leaves the game's damage handling unchanged while Link is sinking in quicksand or lava.

The **Shield Surf R-button speed boost** is an optional menu setting, off by default. While Shield Surfing, hold the game's R button to gradually accelerate from the current speed to the vanilla Ice Leaf maximum of 70 game units per frame, then maintain that speed while R remains held. The boost uses the native 4-unit push acceleration; releasing R restores vanilla speed behavior.

Bow actions are suppressed while Shield Surfing because the vanilla bow process is incompatible with the temporary board-ride state.

When Goron Tunic lava walking is disabled, Link keeps the gradual lava sink and a five-second magma-return delay. The delay restarts if Link leaves the lava, so separate contacts do not accumulate time.

The secondary wheel's close behavior matches the vanilla wheel, including restoring background music and sound-effect levels when the wheel closes.

## Co-op compatibility

The dedicated Ice Leaf slot (`0xFB`) appears immediately left of Shield Surf (`0xFC`) and starts the native Ice Leaf board ride without requiring an equipped shield. Its ride-only actor state leaves the vanilla Ice Leaf pickup behavior untouched. Co-op snapshots include the Shield Surf shield's local rotation and vertical offset; an Ice Leaf ride synchronizes the native board model instead. The **Sword put-away flourish** setting is off by default and routes human Link's ordinary sword put-away through the game's special sword-unequip flourish without changing item put-away or event-controlled unequips.

The Co-op menu includes a warning: this combined build is not compatible with a separate standalone Crests of Courage installation. Disable standalone CoC manually before playing; the Wheel does not unload or modify other mods. All peers must use matching combined builds. The integrated snapshot and actor protocol is version 77.

Room codes, Hyrule Online peer discovery, and Hyrule Online chat use the Wheel's free-tier Cloudflare Worker at `wss://secondary-inventory-wheel-rooms.scp173musicsalt.workers.dev`; set the `room_server` configuration value to use another deployment. The self-hostable Worker source is in `third_party/crests-of-courage/server/`. The shared endpoint now runs the all-player online-name roster protocol; self-hosted endpoints must deploy this source update, since older deployments report totals but cannot provide the roster. Hyrule Online chat is limited to players on the same stage and matching combined-mod version; the Worker does not keep chat history. Room-code co-op chat continues over the existing encrypted peer-to-peer connection. The Worker only exchanges rendezvous and chat data; gameplay snapshots and actor state continue over the existing encrypted peer-to-peer UDP links. The combined mod no longer connects to Remia's CoC chat relay.

The menu bar's **Credits** tab lists the included third-party model creators, Dawnlight's jump, glide, Sprint, aiming, Bullet Time and visual contributions, the Fairy Companion port by AgustinFuac (with permission; abilities omitted), BeZide's Dawnlight Custom Glider texture (with permission), and Co-op source contributors. The full Crests of Courage MIT license and source credits are included in the package under `credits/`; Dawnlight's CC0 1.0 license is also included, and model assets are separately credited.

Manual Jump works for Link and Wolf Link using their native jump actions. It uses Dawnlight's grounded-action checks, keeps the selected facing direction and movement input, supports the native sword jump attack, and scales jump height from 100% to 500%. Glide is independently configurable: press the same jump button while airborne to deploy a spawned Cucco or Dawnlight's cloth Glider. The Glider replaces the spawned Cucco's model; ordinary carried Cuccos are not altered. Glide ends when Link lands or enters an event. With airwalking Hover Boots active, pressing the configured jump button while airborne instead moves the active hover plane to Link's current elevation; at the hover plane it performs a normal manual jump. While Shield Surfing on the Hover Boots plane, the configured jump button starts a native board jump and raises the hover plane as Link rises; the lower-plane binding lowers the plane and lets Link descend without ending the surf. The separate configurable L1/L2/L3/R1/R2/R3 bind lowers the active hover plane by Link's vanilla standing-jump height; it defaults to R3 and lets Link descend to the new plane.

## Ice Arrow

While aiming with Ice Arrow mode enabled, the mod renders an Ice Keese model at the tip of the held arrow, like the bomb model on a bomb arrow. It disappears when the arrow is fired and is not a HUD indicator.

With a bow equipped, hover over the Ice Arrow wheel entry and press R to bind Ice Arrow mode; press R again there to unbind it. The secondary wheel shows the native bow-and-arrow combo prompt only while Ice Arrow is unbound, and shows the combo-off prompt only while it is bound. While bound, pressing R with the bow drawn returns to ordinary arrows; while unbound, the standard bow does not show a switch prompt. Ice Arrow is a temporary bow mode, not an inventory item, and cannot be assigned to X or Y. A hit applies the game's freeze tint and holds the enemy's main model pose and position for up to 90 frames (three seconds), suppressing its attack collider while retaining its target collider so it can still be damaged. The tint is restored when the freeze ends, and killing an enemy immediately releases it so its death and despawn can proceed. Common enemies and bosses are eligible; NPCs, props, shielded hits, and arrow-through targets are excluded. Actors with separately managed secondary models may not freeze every mesh, and projectiles already in flight are unaffected.

## Lens of Truth

The Lens of Truth appears in the secondary wheel directly after Ice Arrow and uses an original, custom icon in the wheel and X/Y HUD. Activate or deactivate it with its assigned X/Y button while in human form; it enables the game's native Wolf Sense vision state and audio, which applies the senses visual effect, and only turns that vision back off if the Lens itself enabled it. While active, it reveals Poes, enables their battle attention flag, and adds non-Wolf attack types to their target colliders so human Link can damage them. The original target masks are restored when the Lens is disabled.

The Stone Mask appears immediately after the Lens of Truth. Its original wheel/HUD icon accompanies a mask model reused from the game's `N_gD_mskF` object archive and rendered on Link's head while the mask is active. The mask also applies best-effort stealth by suppressing the shared actor distance and facing-angle queries that enemy-group actors use to detect Link. Actors and bosses that use direct player-position checks, custom sensing, or other acquisition logic can still detect or attack Link; this is not universal invisibility. Remove the mask by pressing its assigned X/Y button again. It is unavailable in Wolf form.

The Goron Mask appears immediately after the Stone Mask and has an original, low-resolution mask wheel/HUD icon. When equipped, it renders the game's clown mask model on Link's face. While Link is standing or walking, holding B starts a fast native front roll and repeats it continuously until B is released. The roll attacks enemies with Link's native collision sphere, dealing two damage and applying the game's normal hit response. Other actions retain their native animations. Press the assigned X/Y button again to remove it. It is unavailable in Wolf form.

The Giant's Mask appears immediately after the Goron Mask. Its original wheel/HUD icon is paired with the game's mask model from object archive `N_gD_mskF`, resource index 4. Assign it to X or Y and press that button to toggle it; Link's human model renders at twice its normal size while equipped, and sword attacks deal 11 times their normal attack power (1000% more), including Mortal Draw, Great Spin, Ending Blow, and Jump Strike. The multiplier is applied to active sword colliders immediately before the collision pass, covering the different collider setup paths used by hidden skills while preserving normal hit resolution. Co-op and Hyrule Online snapshots carry its active state so remote player bodies also render at twice their normal size. Unequipping it, changing areas, or entering Wolf form restores normal rendering and sword damage. The game has no safe actor-wide size API for Link, so his native movement and collision volumes are not enlarged.

The Armos Hammer appears immediately after the Giant's Mask and uses an original hammer design with stone, brass, and teal details. Assign it to X or Y and press that button to equip or stow it. Equipping requires Link to have his sword; the mod keeps the game's native sword action and collision handling, hides the sword model, and draws the hammer on its live weapon matrix. Press B to swing it with Link's normal sword animations. While equipped, those attacks retain their normal sword type and also carry the bomb attack type, allowing destructible rocks and boulders that check `AT_TYPE_BOMB` to use their native break logic. It is unavailable in Wolf form.

## Sit While Hovering

With airwalking Hover Boots equipped, Sit can be started from the stable hover plane as well as from the ground. Link stays suspended at that height while seated; standing returns him to movement without dropping him.

## Goron Tunic

The Goron Tunic can only be activated while Link wears the Hero's Tunic. Attempts to activate it while wearing other armor, or to equip Zora or Magic Armor from the Collection menu while it is active, play the game's disabled-option sound and are blocked. While active, it prevents other clothing from being equipped and temporarily sets the Hero's Tunic cap, body, and skirt to `#FF0000` through the co-op color system. Deactivating it restores the player's configured Hero's Tunic colors.

Hover Boots airwalking is disabled when Link transforms into Wolf Link. Unequipping Hover Boots while Shield Surfing preserves the active hover-plane ride until the surf ends. The settings menu also has an off-by-default option to make the Spinner slide along walls and steep surfaces instead of reflecting its direction, and a separate option to keep its powered-up attack hitbox active whenever Link is riding it.
